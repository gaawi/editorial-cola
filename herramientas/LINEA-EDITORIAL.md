# ADAR magazine: proposed editorial line

Draft, 25 November 2026. For discussion before the print/online magazine launches next summer. The blog is the testing ground: each section below already has at least one draft post in `src/content/blog/es/`.

## What the magazine is for

ADAR writes from inside the places where it plays: village churches, monasteries, hórreos and paneras, a mountain house in Leiguarda. The magazine should do the same. It reports what happens in rural Asturias when music arrives, stays or gets recorded, and it uses outside reporting (press, essays, reviews) as a starting point for ADAR's own experience, never as a substitute for it.

## Recurring sections

1. **Lugar** (Place). One space per issue: how it sounds, who looks after it, what it was built for, what a concert does to it. Hórreos, monasteries, markets, a field behind a church.
2. **Memoria viva** (Living memory). Intangible heritage and the people who carry it: vaqueiros, tonada, building crafts, rural women's song traditions. Linked to Braña Sonora recordings. Always with the person's consent and a clear credit.
3. **Taller** (Workshop). New music: commissions, the composer in residence, work in progress, scores. What a piece owes to the place where it premieres.
4. **Casa abierta** (Open house). Residencies in Leiguarda 47 and in other villages. What artists leave behind, and what the village gets out of it.
5. **Escucha** (Listening). Acoustic ecology, silence, soundscapes, how a room or valley sounds. Practical listening guides for the audience.
6. **Las cuentas** (The numbers). How a free festival is paid for, who funds rural culture, what cancellations and policy changes mean for small projects. Honest about risks.
7. **Otros pueblos** (Other villages). Short reports on rural festivals and projects elsewhere in Spain and Europe, read from ADAR's point of view.

## Tone

- Plain, short sentences, written by people who were there. Concrete facts (dates, places, names, numbers) over rounded phrases.
- No grand claims. A festival does not repopulate a village; say what it does do, and say what we do not know.
- Cite the outside piece openly, with a link, then move to ADAR's own experience.
- Spanish first, with Asturian words where they are the right words (orbayu, espicha, braña, pegollos).
- Respect for the people quoted or recorded: they review what is published about them.
